# Community code of conduct

Welcome contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, patient, and constructive. Discuss technical ideas using evidence, acknowledge uncertainty, and help first-time contributors understand the project.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, publishing private information, and sustained disruption are unacceptable. Good-faith technical disagreement is welcome; personal attacks are not.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation in the project's spaces. Responses should be proportionate to severity and repetition, with an opportunity to clarify misunderstandings where appropriate. This document does not appoint additional moderators or promise response deadlines.

No private conduct-reporting contact has been designated. The owner must establish one before opening the community to broad participation. Until then, avoid placing private reports or identifying details in public issues. A designated, verified private channel is an outstanding publication decision, recorded in `docs/AUDIT.md`.
